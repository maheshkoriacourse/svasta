Our Story
From a grandmother's kitchen in Kerala to supermarket shelves on four continents.

SVASTA began with one belief: the world's oldest science of wellbeing deserves the world's newest standards of quality. Our founder watched his mother brew ashwagandha and tulsi for every ailment — and asked why this wisdom was missing from modern life.

Today we work directly with 5,000 farmer partners across Kerala, Rajasthan and Uttarakhand, standardise every botanical batch in our NABL-accredited labs, and bottle calm, focus, immunity and rest for the modern world.

3
Serve with respect — recipes tuned for regional tastes, never a one-world formula.
